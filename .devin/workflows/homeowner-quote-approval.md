# Homeowner Quote Approval Flow (Non-Plumbing Services)

## Overview
This workflow documents the steps a homeowner takes to approve and pay for service quotes from providers. This applies to all services except plumbing, which has a two-phase payment structure.

## Steps

1. **Navigate to Service Requests & Quotes**
   - Access the homeowner dashboard
   - Go to the "Service Requests & Quotes" section
   - View "My Service Requests" to find requests with "Quote Sent" status

2. **Review Quote Details**
   - Click on the service request to view full quote details
   - Review the following information:
     - **Timeline**: Expected duration for service completion
     - **Valid Until**: Quote expiration date
     - **Description**: Detailed scope of work
     - **Materials**: List of materials included
     - **Terms & Conditions**: Payment terms, insurance coverage, etc.
     - **Total Cost**: Final quoted

3. **Initiate Approval**
   - If satisfied with the quote, click the "Approve & Pay" button below the quote details
   - This opens the payment modal

4. **Select Payment Method**
   - In the "Payment Details" modal, select preferred payment method:
     - MTN Mobile Money (marked as Default if set)
     - Credit/Debit card (shows last 4 digits)
   - Option to add a new payment method via "+ Add New Card"

5. **Review Payment Summary**
   - Verify the payment breakdown:
     - **Service Cost**: The quoted amount (e.g., RWF 85,000)
     - **Service Fee**: Platform fee (e.g., RWF 1,000)
     - **Amount Due**: Total to be paid (e.g., RWF 86,000)

6. **Confirm Payment**
   - Click the "Pay [Amount]" button to finalize
   - Wait for the "Processing Payment" modal to complete

7. **Payment Confirmation**
   - Success modal appears: "Payment Successful!"
   - Confirmation message: "Your payment of [amount] has been sent to the provider"
   - Next steps displayed:
     - ✓ Provider has received your payment
     - ☐ Please leave a review after the service

8. **Post-Approval**
   - The service request status updates
   - Await service completion from the provider
   - After service completion, leave a review for the provider

## Important Notes

- **Plumbing services have a different flow**: Plumbing quotes require two-phase payment (Pay Materials upfront, Pay Labor after completion)
- **Quote expiration**: Quotes must be approved before the "Valid Until" date
- **Payment security**: All payments are processed securely through the platform
- **Review requirement**: Homeowners are encouraged to leave reviews after service completion

## Example: Removal Service Approval

1. Homeowner receives quote for RWF 85,000 for moving a 2-bedroom apartment
2. Reviews: 1-day timeline, moving truck and materials included, insurance coverage
3. Clicks "Approve & Pay"
4. Selects MTN Mobile Money as payment method
5. Confirms payment of RWF 86,000 (RWF 85,000 + RWF 1,000 service fee)
6. Payment processed successfully
7. Awaits moving service on scheduled date
8. Leaves review after service completion
